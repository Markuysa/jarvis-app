Jarvis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Jarvis is a personal voice assistant for macOS used by its owner on their own Mac. This policy explains what data Jarvis accesses and how it is handled.

Data Jarvis accesses

  • Google account (only after the owner connects it): Google Sheets (to record and summarize the owner's personal finances and to read or update spreadsheets the owner asks about), Google Drive file names (to find spreadsheets by name), Google Calendar (to read and manage the owner's events), YouTube channel data and YouTube Analytics (to report statistics of the owner's own channel).
  • Telegram account (only after the owner connects it): the owner's chats, read and answered on the owner's request.
  • Voice and text requests of the owner. Audio is processed on the Mac and is never uploaded.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out the owner's requests: to answer questions, record entries, build summaries and charts, and manage events. Jarvis never acts on the owner's behalf without a request; sending messages, deleting data and similar irreversible actions require the owner's explicit confirmation.

To compose an answer, the text of the request and the relevant data (for example, the totals of a finance summary) are sent to the Anthropic API (Claude). Anthropic does not use API data to train models by default. No other third parties receive the data.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's Mac. Jarvis has no server of its own and does not store Google or Telegram data anywhere else; spreadsheets and events stay in the owner's Google account. Data is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can disconnect Google or Telegram in Jarvis settings at any time, which deletes the stored token. Google access can also be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Telegram sessions in Telegram → Settings → Devices.
