Jarvis

A personal voice assistant for macOS

Jarvis is a private, single-user assistant that runs on the owner's Mac. It answers voice and text requests, manages the owner's calendar, tasks and notes, and — only when the owner connects them — works with the owner's Google account (Sheets for personal finance tracking, Calendar, YouTube channel statistics) and Telegram account.

Jarvis is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no server that stores user data, and no advertising. Access to any account is granted by the owner in the app's settings and can be revoked at any time.
